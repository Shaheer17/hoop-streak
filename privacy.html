Privacy policy

Hoop Streak · Last updated 21 November 2026

We don't collect your data

Hoop Streak has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your progress stays on your device, and the developer receives no information about you from the game.

What the game saves on your device

Your best score, coins, the balls you own, your daily results and your sound and vibration settings. None of it is sent to the developer. It can be part of your own device backups, which Apple handles under your backup settings. Deleting the game removes everything it saved.

In-app purchases

Coin packs and the Pro Pack are optional and sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and keeps your payment details. The developer never sees your name, card or Apple Account. See the Apple Privacy Policy for how Apple handles this.

What the game doesn't use

Your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location and motion data. The game has no online features.

Children

The game doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

These web pages

The Privacy Policy and Support buttons in the game's Settings open this site in your browser. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process basic technical information such as your IP address when you visit, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. For questions, email shaheeranjum17@gmail.com.
