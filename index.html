How a run plays

Read the hoop

A card in the corner shows how far away the hoop is and how high, in feet. Check it before you pull back, because the next hoop will be somewhere else.

Swish to catch fire

A swish scores more than a shot that touches the rim. Hit three in a row and the ball catches fire for double points, until you make one that isn't a swish or you miss.

Play the daily

Ten shots on a course that is the same for every player that day. Copy your result and send it to a friend.

Spend your coins

Every run pays coins, which buy new balls. Each ball has a perk, like a longer aim guide or one free miss. Perks are off in the daily, so it stays fair.
