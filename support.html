Playing

How do I shoot?
Touch the screen, pull back toward the bottom and let go. Pull further for more power, and to the side to aim. The card in the bottom left shows how far away the hoop is and how high.
What does on fire mean?
Three swishes in a row set the ball on fire, and your makes score double. A make that isn't a swish, or a miss, puts the fire out.
Do ball perks work in the daily?
No. Perks only work in the endless game, so the daily's 10 shots are the same for everyone.
How do I turn off sound or vibration?
Tap the gear on the title screen and switch Sound or Vibrate off.

Progress and purchases

Where is my progress saved?
Only on your iPhone. There is no account or online save, so deleting the game erases your progress and coins. A device backup that includes the game's data can bring them back.
I paid but didn't get my coins
Make sure you're online, then close and reopen the game. Unfinished purchases are delivered the next time it starts. If they still don't arrive, email us with the date of the purchase.
Can I restore what I bought?
The Pro Pack and the Starter Pack, yes: tap Restore Purchases in Settings or in the Coin Store. Coin packs are used up as you spend them, so the App Store can't restore those.
How do I get a refund?
Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
How do I turn off in-app purchases?
On your iPhone, open Settings, then Screen Time, then Content & Privacy Restrictions.
